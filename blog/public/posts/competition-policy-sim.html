1. The Theoretical Foundation

Classical static models assume complete information and instantaneous price adjustment. However, real antitrust enforcement operates under imperfect observation: regulatory audits occur with frequency λ, and discovery probability scales non-linearly with excess margins (P - MC) / P. 

# Sample calibration formulation
def compute_equilibrium_markup(hhi, penalty_rate):
    base_markup = (hhi / 10000.0) * 0.45
    deterrence = 1.0 / (1.0 + np.exp(-0.25 * (penalty_rate - 6.0)))
    adjusted_markup = base_markup * (1.0 - 0.4 * deterrence)
    return max(0.05, adjusted_markup)

2. Key Findings

Our empirical simulations reveal that threshold-based deterrence strategies generate an asymmetric response curve: once enforcement credibility exceeds a critical threshold, tacit collusion drops precipitously without necessitating structural breakups.
