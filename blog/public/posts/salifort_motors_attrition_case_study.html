🌟 Executive Abstract

Employee turnover is not a random occurrence; it is an operational symptom. At Salifort Motors, we initiated a machine learning project to shift Human Resources from a reactive cost center into a proactive workforce-planning unit. 🧹 Section 2: Data Safeguards & De-Biasing

Before fitting estimators, two critical preprocessing gates were established to guarantee real-world inference stability:

  1. Deduplication: Removed 3,008 duplicate rows, creating an honest baseline of distinct employee life cycles.
  2. Data Leakage Prevention: Conventional models rely heavily on subjective satisfaction_level survey results. In production, departing employees either distort or fail to complete exit surveys. We completely omitted satisfaction scores and engineered an objective proxy feature: overworked (logging average monthly hours > 175).
# Feature Engineering & De-biasing Pipeline
import pandas as pd

# 1. Purge duplicate records
clean_df = raw_df.drop_duplicates()

# 2. Drop survey leakage and engineer objective workload proxy
clean_df = clean_df.drop(columns=['satisfaction_level'])

📊 Section 3: Visual Insights & Diagnostic Findings

Exploratory data analysis pinpointed acute structural pressure points across the workforce:

  • The 7-Project Attrition Threshold: 100% of employees juggling 7 projects quit. Individuals handling 6–7 projects logged between 255 and 295 hours per month (~10 hours daily including weekends).
  • Bimodal Attrition Clusters: Departing staff clustered into two contrasting polar cohorts:
    • Underutilized Leavers: Characterized by sub-150 monthly hours and lower evaluation scores (likely performance offboarding).
    • Overworked Burnout Leavers: Characterized by exceptional evaluation scores and extreme hour logs with zero internal growth.
  • The Stagnation Factor: A mere 2.1% of personnel received a promotion in the preceding five years, demonstrating that long working hours were not rewarded with career mobility.

🔮 Section 5: Strategic Operational Action Plan

With feature importances identifying last_evaluation, number_project, tenure, and overworked as primary split drivers, we provided leadership with an operational retention playbook:

  • Workload Ceiling: Enforce an explicit ceiling of 4–5 concurrent projects per employee to eliminate the 100% turnover threshold.
  • Performance Re-alignment: Shift annual review structures from rewarding sheer volume of logged hours toward milestone velocity and execution efficiency.
  • Career Progression Reviews: Establish targeted retention checks and progression paths for staff reaching years 3 through 5 of their tenure.
