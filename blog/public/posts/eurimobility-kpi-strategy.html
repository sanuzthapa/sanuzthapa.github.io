Eurimobility SA: Supply Chain Disruption & Kraljic Rebalancing

Author: Sanuz Thapa
Method: K-Means & Kraljic Matrix
Focus: Risk De-risking & Nearshoring

European automotive and micro-mobility manufacturer Eurimobility SA was over-indexed on tier-1 semiconductor and chassis components sourced from single-region Asian hubs.

1. Bottleneck Items

Microcontrollers & specialty sensors: high supply risk, moderate financial impact. Strategy: Long-term agreements & safety buffers.

2. Strategic Items

Lithium battery cells & custom drivetrains: high risk, high impact. Strategy: Dual-sourcing with local European partners.

3. Non-Critical Items

Fasteners, standard cabling, casing: low risk, low financial impact. Strategy: Catalog standardization & automation.

4. Leverage Items

Aluminum extrusions & stamped metal: low risk, high spend volume. Strategy: Competitive tendering & volume pooling.
